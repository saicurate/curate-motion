**What to make**
1. **New concepts: 6–8 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts,** built around really cool TRANSITIONS and trends. Ideas to explore:
   - match cuts (a phone-photo outfit morphing into the app's floating piece);
   - a whip-pan or swipe between looks;
   - "outfit check" snap transitions;
   - masking through Zee or through the phone frame;
   - 3D phone flips;
   - kinetic-type wipes;
   - a closet-door reveal;
   - a hand-tap-to-transition;
   - "POV: you never know what to wear";
   - a "get ready with me" format;
   - a before/after closet chaos to calm;
   - a day of outfits (Mon–Sun) in one continuous move;
   - a weather-swap transition (rain to sun swaps the look).
   Each one is native-feeling for TikTok and Insta, with a hook in the first 1 s.
2. **The full length spectrum.** For the concepts, cover:
   - **6 s** bumpers;
   - **15 s**;
   - **30 s**;
   - **60 s**;
   - **90 s–2 min** stories.
   Mark which length each concept is made for, and show how the strongest concepts scale (a 6/15/30 cut-down plan).
3. **THE APP EXPLAINER, 2–3 min.** A proper video that explains the whole app, for the website, YouTube and onboarding. Script it scene by scene with a time code:

   - meet Zee;
   - your wardrobe from your photos (the privacy shield, selfie, scan, pieces arriving);
   - pure-float pieces;
   - chat with Zee (asking for an outfit, Zee's thinking, question cards, Adjust, Style notes);
   - weather and location;
   - Try-ons;
   - "Tell me when it's ready";
   - the outro call to action.
   Write a voiceover script (warm and simple; American English), on-screen words (few), the music plan, and the footage needed per scene (from Emma's account).
   - **Voiceover:** note the options (record a human; or TTS, which would cost money and needs the founder's OK, so don't generate it), and DON'T make any paid voice.

   - Put them in cuts/ as c6-motion-test-v1.mp4, explainer-open-v1.mp4 etc., and add them to the tracker as each one renders.
