Make 6 ROUGH DEMO VIDEOS (animatics, not polished renders) of new, hook-first ad concepts for Curate (a fashion stylist iOS app; mascot Zee, a black plush kitten; target US 18–34 on TikTok/Reels).

The founder's feedback on the polished Pop out v1–v3: "music is not so great. and also something is off. i am not feeling it… not creative enough or eye catchy, no hooks as such. u can use other tools… i can get paid APIs as well… so first of all instead of rendering the whole video, give me some thoughts, like a sample demo dummy video with all the transitions and the story."

Goal: judge the IDEA, the HOOK, the STORY and the TRANSITIONS fast. Not polish. Fast pre-G2 exploration in curate-motion; fake placeholders are fine where a real-world shot would later be AI video (grey boxes labelled "AI video shot later").

Six animatics, each 8–15 s, 9:16, 540×960 / 30 fps, with big beat labels in a corner, temp music, rough motion that shows each transition's idea:
1. "Nothing to wear": hook "200 clothes. Nothing to wear." over an overflowing messy closet; she sighs, opens Curate, her clothes fly OUT of the phone and hang themselves on a rail; end "Curate. Your closet, finally sorted."
2. "Your camera roll is secretly a closet": fast thumb-scroll through her REAL camera-roll photos; hard freeze on the beat; the outfit peels off her into 3D and lands in a closet grid.
3. "Zee the critic": Zee walks in, looks her outfit up and down, unimpressed meme reaction ("girl… no"); swaps the pieces with a paw tap; "slay". Meme format, funny.
4. "Speed run": "0 → 49 pieces in 3 minutes", huge ticking timer and counter; pieces rain into the grid, accelerating; ends on the full closet.
5. "Outfit-change trend": a hand covers the lens on the beat and the outfit changes, 3 times; each look from the app's real looks, with Zee's pick shown.
6. "Satisfying sort": a chaotic pile of her pieces snaps into a perfect colour-sorted closet, ASMR snaps on each beat.

For each: the hook (first 1 s), beat-by-beat story, transitions list, which shots are AI video later (Veo/Kling/Seedance) vs code motion vs real app footage, music direction (tempo, mood, reference style), why it would stop the scroll.

Music: the Mixkit tracks feel generic to the founder. Pick the best temp track per concept, or find better FREE commercially licensed tracks (check each licence exactly) and record licences. Prepare (do NOT run) paid options with costs: ElevenLabs Music API (Starter), and a licensed library (Artlist / Epidemic Sound). No paid generation without the founder's OK.

Output: marketing/videos/cuts/demo-<n>-<slug>-v1.mp4; code in curate-motion/ads/_demos/<slug>/ (registered, status draft, gate "explore"); ONE phone page artifact (copy tools/review-page/ads-phone.html style; icon "video") with each demo's concept, hook, beats, transitions, AI-video shots and music direction, plus a "Paid options" box. No paid calls, no secrets printed, no git commits.
