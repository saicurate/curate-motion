# S-V3 brief: the Essembl theme, product-led

**Why:** the founder rejected S-V1 (loud colour), S-V1 v2 (quiet palette) and S-V2 (editorial). On the
reference board (18 fashion apps' real App Store sets) he picked Essembl: "I liked the theme of this but
don't do editorial for me."

**The theme, learned not copied (inspo.competitor.essembl-redesign.v1):** a warm cream linen ground with
soft light; huge tight two-line headlines, ink then one deep muted tone; a big phone tilted and running off
the edge, the real UI on it; real garments in front of and around it with real contact shadows; one idea per
screenshot; no people as the subject. Their layouts and words are theirs; ours below.

**Hard lines:** real build-284 screens only (her data, probe captures); Emma's own wardrobe items; no fake
ratings, laurels or press (our real rating is 5.0 from 4 ratings, too few to show); no prices or shops
(RULES 2); the phone is Curate's generic 3D phone (decisions/0012), never called an iPhone.

## Copy (two lines, the strongest first; ✓ = used)

| # | Outcome | Options |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | value prop: Today | ✓ "Today's look, / already picked." · "Dressed in / one tap." · "Know what / to wear today." |
| 2 | the scan | ✓ "Your closet, / from your photos." · "Your whole closet, / no photo shoot." · "Every item you own, / found for you." |
| 3 | try-on | ✓ "See it on you, / before you wear it." · "See it on you, / first." · "Try it on / without the mirror." |
| 4 | weather | ✓ "Rain or shine, / you're dressed." · "Whatever the weather, / covered." · "Dressed for / the forecast." |
| 5 | the stylist | ✓ "Your stylist, / one message away." · "Ask Zee / what to wear." · "Ask your stylist / anything." |
| 6 | privacy | ✓ "Your photos, / your call." · "Only the photos / you choose." |

Rejected on purpose: anything that echoes Essembl's lines ("…nothing to wear?", "Dressed for any plan",
"…in your pocket").

## Type
Geist, two weights rendered: Medium (500, RULES 8 "titles Medium") and SemiBold (600). Side by side with
Essembl at 25%, SemiBold holds the headline better; it is offered for comparison, Medium is the rule.


## 10-24 · calm layouts

Founder: "tilted [phones] is fine, the wardrobe items being thrown is not fine." Same blue linen, headlines, phone and
margins; six layouts O1–O6 for screenshots 1–3, picks key `layout`. O2 (phone + tidy row) and O5 (big phone, cropped)
rendered in full, as the most product-led and the most readable at 25%. Copy: the same six headlines; the garment rows
are the pieces on each screen (S1 blazer · cami · skirt · boots; S2 coat · denim jacket · scarf; S3 tee · tote · loafers;
S4 trench · dark jeans · tote · boots; S5 cami · skirt · boots).
