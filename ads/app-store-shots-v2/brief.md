# Brief: App Store screenshots, set S-V2 (editorial)

**FILM (the set in one sentence):** six bright magazine pages that each say one outcome Curate gives Emma, with her real photos, her own clothes and the real app.

- **Audience:** someone comparing outfit apps in App Store search; they see the first three as a row at about 25%.
- **Format:** iPhone 6.9", 1320 × 2868 PNG, no alpha (`store-shot`), frame 0 of `app-store-shots-v2--store-shot--6s`, props `{"shot": 1–6}`.
- **Principles** (Scrnsht Studio, pasted by the founder 2026-10-24): screen 1 = value prop; the outcome, not the feature; one message each; the idea readable with the copy hidden; a real person in every frame; trust only from what's true; short, oversized headlines.
- **Messages:** 1 Always know what to wear. · 2 Your closet, from your photos. · 3 See it on you. · 4 A look for every day. · 5 Ask your stylist. · 6 Your photos stay yours. (the app's own privacy-shield title; the three lines under it are the shield's, word for word).
- **ASSETS:** Emma's camera roll (approved), her garment cut-outs (approved), the build-284 Wardrobe and Studio captures (draft), Apple's iPhone 17 Pro Max bezel, Zee's happy still, Geist.
- **VISUAL:** white paper, a caps kicker, the headline at 140–160 px (the key phrase Primary Black, the rest grey), one big photo or a grid of photos, garments standing on the page with their contact shadows, the phone straight on with nothing over it. Colour only from the photos.
- **HARD LINE:** no ratings, reviews, awards, prices, shops or brands; nothing the app doesn't do; the bezel untouched (Apple's rules); Emma never vouches for a product.
