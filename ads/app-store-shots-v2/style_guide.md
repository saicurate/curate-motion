# Style guide: set S-V2 (editorial)

- **Page:** white (C.card) fading to Mist ground (C.ground) at the foot. 96 px margins; content 1128 px wide.
- **Kicker:** Geist SemiBold 30 px, +0.18em, caps, C.t3, at y 170: "01 · Today", "02 · Wardrobe", "03 · Studio", "04 · This week", "05 · Your stylist", "06 · Privacy".
- **Headline:** Geist Medium 140–160 px, −0.045em, line-height 0.98, from y ≈ 234. Key phrase C.ink, the rest C.t3 (the front door's two tones). Never bold.
- **Photos:** rounded 22–40 px, cover-cropped around Emma, lit as prints (two soft shadows). Rotated a few degrees only when stacked as prints. Never painted colour fields: the colour is the photos'.
- **Garments:** `<Piece>` cut-outs standing on the page with their floor shadow; optional plain-name caption in Geist 25 px C.t2 (her own clothes, never a brand).
- **Phone:** `<Device>` iPhone 17 Pro Max silver, straight on, ≥ 500 px wide, the build-284 screen as captured. Nothing drawn over the bezel; photos may sit behind it.
- **Chat (5):** her bubble white with a lifted shadow, right; Zee's bubble Primary Black with white text, left, Zee small (150 px) beside it. Zee's line names only her own pieces.
- **Read test:** the first three side by side at 330 px wide (25%) must keep every headline legible.
