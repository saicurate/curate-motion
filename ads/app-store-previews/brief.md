# App Store previews (3), build 284

**FILM:** three App Previews for the screenshot row, one feature each, cut from real
screen recordings of build 284 (the new look) on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator, with
short two-tone captions in the S-V3 theme.

**Why:** the founder, 2026-10-24: "iOS allows videos under the photos section as well…
let's generate videos for the previews as well." The header film is a separate ad.

**Audience:** someone comparing wardrobe apps in the App Store, sound off.

**Apple's rules (App Store Connect reference + App Review 2.3.4):** 886 × 1920 portrait
for the 6.9"/6.5" iPhone; 15–30 s; ≤ 30 fps; H.264 10–12 Mbps or ProRes 422 HQ; a stereo
AAC track (silent is fine); ≤ 500 MB; poster frame defaults to 5 s. Footage must be screen
captures of the app itself; text overlays and light motion graphics are allowed. No fake UI.

**The three:**
1. **"Your closet, from your photos."** Extract all: her photos sorted, the items found in
   them, the items landing in her Wardrobe rails.
2. **"See it on you."** Studio: her looks on the board, the try-on on her photo.
3. **"Always know what to wear."** Today's look card, then asking her stylist in Chat and the
   look it picks.

**Footage:** real build-284 code (release/1.5.1-284 @8ef12d4a) through the dev probes
(new_look_probe, new_look_chat_probe): every server call faked, no prod account, no paid
calls; the scan's photos are the founder's faceless gallery set, the closet the study's.

**VISUAL:** S-V3's world (warm cream, linen ground, Geist, deep muted second tone). The
recording sits in Apple's iPhone 17 Pro Max bezel (`<Device>`), straight on, with the
caption above it, never on it. A 3 s hook: the caption and the app already moving.

**HARD LINE:** only what the app really shows; nothing comes out of the screen; no ratings,
no prices, no claims the app doesn't make.
