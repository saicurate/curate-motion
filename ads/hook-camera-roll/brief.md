- **Audience:** US 18–34 on TikTok / Reels / Shorts who have never heard of Curate. They all have thousands of photos of themselves.
- **Duration:** this is the HOOK, the first 8 s of the ad (`lengths: [8]`); the full cut carries on from its last frame (the jacket floating).
