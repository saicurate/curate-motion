The App Store header, as films

Every direction and variant for iOS 27’s product-page header (21:9) and search card (3:2), made in the motion studio: her real photos and wardrobe, the real build-284 screens in Apple’s iPhone bezel, Geist type, real light and shadow. The App Store takes one header video, so the one film (30 s, all three ideas) comes first; the single films (15 s each) follow. Pick one or more; the picks go to G3 and then the final cut.

Sharp: full-size stills, shown at twice their size on this page, with 100% crops under each variant so the detail can be judged.
Subtle colour: A2 and B3 are tonal (each set’s colour taken from her photos and pushed close to Mist, one hero at a time). The loud colour-blocked versions sit at the end of their direction, marked superseded, still pickable.
Real: every photo, cut-out, try-on and app screen is ours (Emma is our AI persona). The screens are build 284 on her data. Not uploaded to the App Store; it would ship with the new-look App Store release. Zee’s film is parked.

The one film

The App Store takes one header video and one search card, so the directions become one 30 s loop in the subtle palette: A (her photos give up the coat, jacket and scarf; her real Wardrobe) → B (the black tee onto her photo; Studio’s real try-on) → C (a week of looks) → the Curate lockup → back to the first frame. The first 5 s land on their own. Every beat arrives on the same slow camera glide through a soft column of light: one camera through one studio.
