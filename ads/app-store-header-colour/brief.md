# App Store header, direction D "Colour story": brief (G1)

**FILM:** Her whole wardrobe, by colour: one piece at a time, each in its own quiet tone, then all of them
in one place, her real Wardrobe.

**Why this one, and why quiet:** the founder, 2026-10-24: "really good quality stuff… a few more versions…
a bit more colorful"; then, on the colourful cut: "feeling cheap… the colours please subtle colours". So
the hand-in is tonal: oat, sand, blush, sage, dusty blue, stone, pushed toward Mist, never a rainbow; one
hero at a time, a lot of air, soft real shadows. The bar: the Essembl App Store set
(`inspo.competitor.essembl-redesign.v1`, grammar only). The loud first cut stays as versions (d1-bold,
d2-bold).

**Audience:** someone looking at Curate's App Store page or its search card, sound off, for 2–3 seconds.

**Duration / formats:** a 15 s seamless loop, 60 fps (the App Store plays 30 from the same timeline), muted.
`store-header` 3840 × 1646 (21:9) and `store-search` 2880 × 1920 (3:2), one scene in H units: what matters
stays inside ±0.72 H.

**The hand-in (quiet):**
- **d1 · Tonal rooms.** A dolly through six quiet rooms, each a seamless sweep in one tone with one hero
  standing in it (oat knit, sand coat, blush slip dress, sage flannel, dusty-blue denim jacket, stone
  blazer), its name in the left column. Then the stone room shrinks into the sixth tile of a palette,
  the other five fly in, and her real Wardrobe (build 284) fades up on a silver iPhone: "Every colour you
  own, *in one place.*" Back: the oat tile opens into the oat room (= frame 0).
- **d2 · Tonal row.** One hero at a time on a tonal sweep; as the next arrives, the last steps aside into
  a small row; the ground takes each hero's tone. Then the six stand on their colour chips beside her real
  Wardrobe on a silver iPhone. Back: the row leaves and the oat knit returns to the stage (= frame 0).

**The versions (bold, kept):** d1-bold (rich colour rooms, three pieces each, Deep Blue iPhone) and
d2-bold (a colour carousel with a coloured glow, then six colour strips, Cosmic Orange iPhone).

**ASSETS:** her pieces as v2 cut-outs (full resolution, made locally from the originals with BiRefNet,
`tools/cutout-person`; drafts superseding the approved v1s), the build 284 Wardrobe capture
`screenshot.emma.b284-wardrobe-rails.v1`, Apple's iPhone 17 Pro Max bezel (Silver; Deep Blue and Cosmic
Orange for the bold versions), Geist. No people, no paid generation.

**HARD LINE:** no prices, no shopping, no logos, no pipeline; the phone is Apple's bezel straight on,
nothing enters or leaves its screen; the last frame is the first.
