Camera and transitions

  • One camera. It glides back, glides in and pushes in. It never shakes, orbits or whips.
  • No cuts and no crossfades between scenes. Shots join by the glide and by matched shapes: the roll's tile is the app's photo card.
  • Inside the phone, one real screen rises over the other.
  • Real footage at real speed; nothing drawn or edited inside the app.

Rhythm and sound

  • Beats measured on the track itself: 130.00 BPM, ±8 ms.
  • Changes on beats, big moments on downbeats, whooshes on the claps into each bar.
  • Sound made in code on the same clock: a soft kick, a pop for each lift, a riser into 41, a chime on the CTA. −14 LUFS.
  • The sound is judged blindfolded first, from the audio and its waveform.
