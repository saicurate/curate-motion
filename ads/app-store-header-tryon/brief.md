# App Store header, film B "See it on you": brief (G2)

**FILM:** before anyone reads a word, show Curate putting a piece of clothing on *her* photo.

**What it is:** film B of the iOS 27 App Store product-page **header** (21:9, 3840 × 1646) and
**search-results card** (3:2, 2880 × 1920): a 15 s **muted** seamless loop, 60 fps (30 for App Store
Connect if it asks). v2 of `ads/app-store-header`'s `tryon` film, made in parallel with that ad on
2026-10-24 (the founder: run the directions in parallel); that folder keeps film A (roll) and the review
page, and is untouched here. The redo of studies/ob172 (HTML mockups, rejected: "all are cheap quality").
Ships with the new-look App Store release (the App Store still has 1.5.0, the old look).

**Audience:** someone on Curate's App Store page or in search results, sound off. On a phone the header
is about 393 × 168 pt: the words are ≥ 8.8% of the frame's height in the header (≈ 15 pt there) and the
search card is re-laid out (tighter, never cropped).

**The film (15 s):**
- HOOK 0–2.7 s: her walk-to-work photo as a big lit print, the black tee hovering beside it;
  "See it *on you.*" (up from frame 0: the poster frame says what the film does).
- REVEAL 2.7–6.1 s: the tee lifts, flies onto her and lands on her chest; a light edge runs down the
  print and above it she's wearing it: Studio's real try-on (the same street, the same bag, only the
  striped top turns black, registered to the pixel). The camera pushes in and the street moves behind her
  (her cut-out over the plate behind her: two layers, real parallax).
- PROOF 6.1–11.3 s: the print slides aside and the phone rises with Studio showing that same try-on;
  "Before you *wear it.*"
- LOOP 11.3–15 s: the phone sinks, the print returns, the edge runs back up (her stripes come back), the
  tee lifts off her and floats back to its place; "See it *on you.*" lands again. Frame 899 = frame 0.

**VISUAL:** a seamless Mist studio sweep lit from the upper left; the photo as a print with a sheen and
two shadows falling right and down, standing just above the floor (a soft contact shadow); the tee as a
cut-out with its own shadow and one on the floor while it rests; Apple's bezel straight on (x, y,
scale, opacity only) with a soft shadow; Geist Medium, the key phrase in ink, the rest grey; fine grain
and a light vignette.

**HARD LINE:** no invented screens or numbers (the phone shows the real Studio screen, the print the
real try-on); no prices, shopping or store names; never show the pipeline (the light edge is a flourish,
not a "processing" bar); no logos (the tee and her clothes are logo-free); ≤ 4+ content.
