# App Store header, direction C: "A week of looks"

**FILM:** a look for every day, from your own wardrobe.

**Audience:** someone on Curate's App Store page or in search results on iOS 27: muted, a few seconds,
deciding whether this app does something for *them*.

**Duration:** one 15 s seamless loop (the header and the search card both loop, muted). No cut-downs.

**Formats:** `store-header` 3840 × 1646 (21:9, the product-page header) and `store-search` 2880 × 1920
(3:2, the search card), from the same timeline. Everything that matters sits inside ±0.72 H of the
centre, so neither crop cuts it.

**The film:** Monday to Sunday, a look from Emma's own wardrobe each day, laid flat on its own set:
Mon Work, Tue Coffee, Wed Gallery, Thu Errands, Fri Dinner, Sat Market, Sun Slow day. Then the whole
week as seven cards, "A look for *every day.*" and the Curate lockup. Back to Monday.
- **c1 flip:** each day is the whole frame; the next set glides in from the right (the camera spring),
  its pieces landing and its name rising once it has arrived. The week forms by Sunday shrinking into
  its card and the others rising behind it; Monday's card zooms back to the whole frame.
- **c2 rail:** the seven sets are cards on a rail; the camera glides a card a step. The active card is
  the hero; the neighbours step back (smaller, fainter). The camera pulls back to the whole week, then
  pushes back into Monday.

**Tone:** `subtle` (handed in; the founder 2026-10-24: "subtle colours"): each day's paper is a pale
tone from that look's own palette pushed toward Mist, dark type, soft shadows, one hero at a time.
`bold` (kept as a version): the first pass, fashion-campaign paper colours.

**ASSETS:** 30 of Emma's wardrobe cut-outs (`garment.emma.*`, approved; see ad.json), the Curate lockup
(`brand.curate.logo-splash.v1`), Geist. No app screens (none is invented); no photos; nothing generated.

**VISUAL:** a still, lit paper set per day (a top-left key light, a soft window-light diagonal that
drifts and loops, the paper's fall-off, a seeded paper tooth), the pieces laid flat with two shadows
(contact + soft, down-right), never drawn above their native size (sharp at 4K). Geist Medium for the
day; the occasion in Winter Gray.

**HARD LINE:** no prices, shopping or brands; no invented product screens or numbers; never show the
pipeline; Emma is a persona (AI-generated), disclosed in ad.json.
