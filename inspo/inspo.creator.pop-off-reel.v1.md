**What it is.** A creator's "how to: Pop Off" tutorial for Meta's Edits app (filmed in Dubai), sent by the founder on WhatsApp. Not an ad and not a fashion reference: only the 3.8-second opening is worth taking. Full breakdown with contact sheets: the phone page linked above.

**The hook (0.00–3.83 s).** A top-down still of an iPhone on a closed laptop shows her Instagram grid. Cut-out versions of her in four outfits rise out of the screen one at a time, pose with live motion, and shrink back in: each scales from about 15% to 100% in about 0.2 s with overshoot, holds about 0.5 s, then returns. The next outfit starts rising before the last has gone, so there's never an empty beat (one outfit per 2 beats at 129 BPM). "how to: Pop Off" types on letter by letter at 1.0–1.6 s, in a white, tall, condensed display face, behind her head. Hard cut on the kick.

**The rest (3.83–32.3 s).** Step 1 jump cuts on a rooftop; step 2 photos flash white and drop in as tilted prints; an Edits screen recording (scan dots, a yellow outline traced round the cut-out, hand-drawn ellipses circling buttons); the export ends inside a rounded phone outline with a gradient stroke tracing the frame.
