# Jake: persona sheet (skit ads, 2026-10-16)

A made-up guy for Curate's US story-time skit ads (first one: `ads/skit-chopped`). Not a real
person, and he never vouches for any product or merchant (RULES.md › 5). He shows his own clothes
and uses the app; he never says a piece is "worth it", "true to size" or similar. He may react to
Curate's styling ("ok that's actually clean").

## Who he is
- **Name:** Jake (no surname on screen). 20.
- **Lives:** Bloomington, Indiana: a sophomore at the big state school there, sharing a
  two-bedroom off-campus apartment with a roommate. Bikes or walks to class.
- **Grew up:** a suburb of Indianapolis. First in his friend group to have a car, a 2011 sedan.
- **Studies:** kinesiology. Works part-time at the front desk of the campus rec center.
- **Weekends:** pickup basketball, the rec gym, tailgates, frozen pizza and video games, grocery
  runs at the big supermarket on the edge of town.
- **The problem Curate solves for him:** he has clothes, he just has no idea what goes with what.
  He dresses like he got dressed in the dark, every day, and nobody has told him.

## Build (fixed for every candidate)
- **Height:** 6'2" (188 cm). **Build:** nicely built: broad shoulders, athletic from basketball
  and the gym, not bodybuilder-huge. US size L tops, 32x32 jeans.
- **Looks:** average. A normal, friendly face; not a model, not ugly. A little awkward: an eager
  grin that's slightly too big, a nervous laugh. Clean-shaven or light stubble. No tattoos.
- **The joke is the OUTFIT, never his looks.** He is likeable; we laugh with him.

## Face: three candidates (the founder picks one)
- **A:** light skin with a warm undertone and a few faint freckles; grey-blue eyes; short,
  slightly messy sandy-brown hair.
- **B:** warm tan skin (Mexican-American); dark-brown eyes; thick black hair, short on the sides
  and a little longer and messy on top; the faint shadow of a first mustache.
- **C:** medium-dark brown skin (Black American); brown eyes; a short low-cut fade; a soft,
  boyish face with a wide smile.

## His style, before (the bad pieces: everything he owns)
Nothing fits, nothing goes together, and he wears it with total confidence.
- Oversized faded graphic tees (a cracked flame print, a loud tie-dye), a stretched mustard tee.
- Baggy knee-length khaki cargo shorts, shiny mesh basketball shorts, ripped baggy light-wash jeans.
- A boxy striped polo, a too-big flame-print button-up, a pilled giant hoodie, a 90s colour-block
  windbreaker, pleated too-long khakis.
- White crew socks pulled up with black slides; chunky grey dad sneakers.
- A camo flat-brim cap, worn backwards.

## His style, after (the good pieces Curate styles him in)
Clean, fitted, simple: fitted dark-indigo jeans, a white oxford shirt, a clean black bomber, white
leather sneakers, a navy crewneck sweater, a heavyweight white tee, an olive overshirt, stone chinos,
brown suede Chelsea boots, a brown leather belt, a fitted black tee.
