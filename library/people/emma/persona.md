# Emma: persona sheet (marketing videos, 2026-10-15)

A made-up woman for Curate's US ads. Not a real person, and she never vouches for any
product or merchant (FTC note in seed-population-program). She shows her own clothes and
uses the app; she never says a piece is "worth it", "true to size" or similar.

## Who she is
- **Name:** Emma (no surname on screen). 27.
- **Lives:** Astoria, Queens, in a two-bedroom she shares with a friend. Commutes to Manhattan on the N/W.
- **Grew up:** Columbus, Ohio. Moved to New York at 22 after college (Ohio State, graphic design).
- **Job:** junior designer at a small branding studio in the Flatiron District. Office is
  business-casual, Fridays are jeans.
- **Weekends:** farmers market, coffee with friends, long walks in Brooklyn, a beach day at
  Rockaway in summer, one big night out a month.
- **Money:** careful. Buys a few good pieces a season and re-wears them a lot. The problem
  Curate solves for her: "I have clothes, I just never know what to wear them with."

## Build (fixed for every candidate)
- **Height:** 5'6" (168 cm). **Build:** average, slim-to-medium, US size 6.
- Natural brows, little makeup (tinted moisturiser, mascara, lip balm). Short natural nails.
- Small gold hoops most days. No tattoos visible.

## Face: three candidates (the founder picks one)
- **A:** light skin with a warm undertone and light freckles across the nose; hazel eyes;
  shoulder-length wavy dark-blonde / light-brown hair, often half-up in a claw clip.
- **B:** light-medium olive skin; brown eyes; long dark-brown hair with a soft natural wave,
  usually down with a middle part.
- **C:** medium-brown skin (mixed Black and white heritage); brown eyes; shoulder-length
  natural curls (3B), often pushed back with a thin headband or loose.

## Her style (approved mix)
- **Weekdays (NYC minimal):** black, white, grey, camel, navy. Blazer + white tee +
  straight-leg trousers, oxford shirt + midi skirt, trench over everything, loafers or
  ankle boots, a black tote.
- **Weekends (casual):** straight-leg jeans, grey crewneck sweatshirt or hoodie, flannel,
  denim jacket, white leather sneakers or retro runners, baseball cap, crossbody.
- **Going out (one dressy look):** little black dress or the slip dress, heels, mini bag,
  gold hoops.
- Seasons: puffer and beanie in winter, cable-knit and wool coat in fall, sandals and a
  tank in summer.

## Wardrobe
About 50 pieces, listed in `../garments/manifest.json` (product photos, no logos).
