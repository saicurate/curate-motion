# App inventory: Curate 1.5.1 (284), the new look

Every screen and animation a user can reach in build 284 (release/1.5.1-284 @8ef12d4a; 1.7.0 (285) is the same content), read from the code on 2026-10-24 and mapped against build 262's inventory (`INVENTORY.md`): SAME / CHANGED / NEW / GONE, how to capture each one (which `lib/dev` probe, or a real account and what it costs) and its ad value. Two parts: the first run (cold launch to the hand-off into the tabs) and the main tabs. Nothing was run on a device; check before relying on a capture note.

Read this before capturing app footage: about 25 older probes in lib/dev still draw v1 screens (see each part's probe notes), and footage from them is not the new look (tag it `look:v1`).

---

## First run: cold launch → the hand-off into the tabs

Build 284 (`release/1.5.1-284` @8ef12d4a; 285 is the same content). Code paths are relative to `lib/`. The old inventory is `curate-motion/library/footage/INVENTORY.md` (build 262 @5aff9592; that commit is in this repo, so every "vs 262" below is from `git diff 5aff9592 HEAD`, not from memory).

**What a new user can actually reach.** `kOnlyNewLook = true` (`core/theme/app_layout.dart:38`) is applied in `app/bootstrap.dart:92-102`, and `LayoutController.build` returns v2 from the first frame (`features/look/layout_controller.dart:82`, `:107`). Three things shape the run:
- **The way in** (front door, email forms, the code screen, onboarding) still wears **mist**, as it did in 262 (`features/look/way_in_look.dart`).
- **The app past the way in** wears **soft**: Geist on the Mist ground.
- **Sign-up is the short v18 run.** Its manifest comes from the server. Per the build notes, onboarding-service serves v18 to builds ≥ `ONBOARDING_SHORT_SIGNUP_MIN_BUILD=280`, so 284 gets v18. The app only checks the manifest's shape (`ShortSignup.isShort`, `features/onboarding/data/short_signup.dart:66-70`). If the server ever served v17 again, the old long run would come back as drawn in 262.

### 1. Summary

**284.** The run goes in this order:
1. **Splash.** The same pulsing mark, now on the Mist ground.
2. **Front door.** The same type reel. "Get started" brings the Apple / Google / email rows out under the hairline, but the reel now rests on "Wear it." instead of rolling to "Sign in." While an event draw is live there is a "Have an event code?" link. A saved code turns the wordmark into an event card.
3. **Sign up.** Apple or Google (native sheets), or email, which uses the same sign-up form and 6-digit code screen as 262. If a code was saved, the draw's "Your ticket is waiting" opens over onboarding.
4. **Two onboarding screens, in Geist on Mist.**
   - **01 / 02 "Nice to meet you."** The old screen: name, Woman / Man, and Birthday side by side, with the birthday wheel already open.
   - **02 / 02 username.** The same self-checking rows and the same stamp-and-fly into the name chip. The button reads **Done**.
5. **"Saving your answers"**, then a brief "Curate" splash.
6. **New: "Welcome, Ava."** Zee waves inside a white glow, wearing a tilted name tag she can rename. Then "Let's go".
7. **Wardrobe tab, empty.** The router sends anyone who owed onboarding there. It shows "Nothing here yet", the static Extract all card and Add one row, and the new glass capsule (Today · Wardrobe · Chat · Studio · Settings).
8. **Start.** First the sheet "First, a selfie". Next the camera, which opens already open: "Look straight at the camera", blink, "That's you.". Then the sheet "Now, your photos", with Continue. Then iOS's Allow Full Access alert.
9. **The Wardrobe tab becomes the scan**, on white. The sort deals photos onto "not you" / "of you" piles. Then her photos turn in a fan while her items are drawn. Then the landing: the prints sink, the rails rise, and the band reads "Added 14 wardrobe items from 32 photos". Throughout, a ring fills on the Wardrobe glyph, the page offers "Tell me when it's done", and first-day tips may appear.

**262.** Same splash and reel. Getting the doors out rolled the reel to "Sign in.". Then a six-step onboarding: 01 About, 02 Username, 03 This-or-that bracket, 04 Budget card flip, 05 the Mirror selfie colour reading ("Deep Autumn"), 06 the 3D body with tapes. Then the empty Wardrobe with Zee asking and answering. Then the privacy shield (Zee peeks), the full-screen selfie step (ring, Zee peek, camera, "That's you! ✨", "There you are"), and photo access. Then the full-screen scan stage (deck riffle, roll with Zee's lens, photos flipping into Picked, "Found 30 photos of you") and the pieces wait (her photo up big, Zee lines, style facts, first piece lift). Then "Tell me" and the notification ask. Then the "Curate at work" pill and "Your wardrobe is ready ›", Zee's cheer, and the review (count-up, remove/undo, Add to wardrobe).

**None of the 262 path after the empty Wardrobe is on a 284 new user's normal path.** It can still play through one edge path; see §4.

**Counts** (this scope; rows marked "native" are counted in SAME):
- **SAME: 10.** Two of them were never listed in 262: the native Apple/Google sheets and the iOS Photos alert.
- **CHANGED: 12.** The beat exists in 284 but looks or moves differently, so the 262 footage is stale.
- **NEW: 34.** That is 13 in the sign-up and scan, plus 21 Event Draw moments. Some of the new ones replace a 262 beat; each row says which.

### 2. Tables, step by step

**Probe key.** Every probe below fakes all server calls and costs $0 unless a row says otherwise.
- **OM** = `onboarding_mist_probe.dart --dart-define=PROBE_CONTROL=<file>`. Write a location to the file.
- **SS** = `short_signup_probe.dart --dart-define=PROBE_CONTROL=<file>`. The real OnboardingScreen on the v18 fixture. Control lines: `run:rest|typed|ready`, `tap:<Key>`, `text:<words>`, `type:<handle>`. Its `wheel` line taps a key from the unused ShortNameStage, so open the wheel by tapping instead.
- **NL** = `new_look_probe.dart --dart-define=CURATE_LAYOUT=v2 --dart-define=PROBE_TODAY=1 --dart-define=PROBE_EXTRACT=true --dart-define=PROBE_CONTROL=<file>`. Add `PROBE_TIPS=true` and/or `PROBE_STYLIST=Zee` where a row says so. This is the real app (router, AppShell, the glass capsule). Get an empty closet with `today:empty`, then go to `/wardrobe`.
- **NC** = `new_look_chat_probe.dart --dart-define=CURATE_LAYOUT=v2 --dart-define=PROBE_CONTROL=<file>`.
- **ED** = `event_draw_probe.dart --dart-define=PROBE_V2=true --dart-define=screen=<scene>`. Without PROBE_V2 you get the classic bar.

**What a real new account costs.** Sign-up, onboarding, the welcome rename and the draw's own endpoints spend no AI. But every real run needs a brand-new account: the welcome shows once per account per phone, and the draw is for new accounts only.

In 284 the new look is forced on (`kOnlyNewLook = true`, `core/theme/app_layout.dart:38`, applied in `app/bootstrap.dart:89-101`): the whole app is Geist on white and Mist, light only, under a floating glass capsule of five glyphs, **Today · Wardrobe · Chat · Studio · Settings** (`features/shell/presentation/shell_tabs.dart:92-100`, `app_shell.dart:101-116`), and the app opens on Today (new people and empty wardrobes open on Wardrobe). **Today** is new: the day's looks, made on the server from her wardrobe items, each laid flat on a band with Try it on, Save, Open in Studio and Not for me, plus the greeting, the weather, an Ask Zee bar and the app's only notifications bell. **Wardrobe** is now only her wardrobe items, as sideways rails per shelf with count chips, search and a black + (Take a photo / Pick a photo / Extract all); the week, calendar, Create outfit and Your outfits have left the tab and are opened from elsewhere. **Chat** is still the stylist chat with no tab bar, redrawn as a white page with Zee in black bubbles; while a look is made a full-width ring of her own wardrobe items turns, looks land as flat lays with an "Open in Studio" action, and most of Zee's animated appearances in chat became still faces. **Studio** replaces the Try-ons tab, which can't be reached in 284 (`/tryons` and `/profile/tryons` both go to Studio): a board of her saved looks on occasion "walls" that she swipes between, filled from a wardrobe tray, with items lifted and swapped, items brought in from a link, and a try-on that passes a mesh over her photo (the 262 curtain is not used here) and opens on a new result card; Zee now sits on the tab bar on every tab but Chat. **Settings** is the Profile branch with her face as its glyph. It still opens on a profile page, now with looks · try-ons · items counts, colours / body / city cards and Looks · Try-ons · Gallery tabs, and the grouped Settings list is behind the gear. Posts, followers, try-on history and dark mode are gone.
