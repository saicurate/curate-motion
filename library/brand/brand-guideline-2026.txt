07   Mission & Vision
Brand Mission                  Curate was born out of frustration. After ordering multiple pants
                               online that didn’t fit, we realized how broken the online
                               shopping experience truly is.  

                               What started as a casual conversation soon turned into a
                               shared mission to solve this problem at scale. Five months ago,
                               we began building Curate with features like virtual try-on and
                               smart size guidance.  

                               Today, Curate is a fully functional product that removes the
                               guesswork from online shopping and helps customers shop
                               with confidence.




Brand guidelines   Our Story                                                                  05
Tone of voice                      Curate communicates with clarity, confidence, and simplicity. Our
                                   tone is modern, approachable, and human, making advanced
                                   technology feel effortless and easy to understand.  

                                   We avoid unnecessary complexity and focus on helping users
                                   make smarter shopping decisions with confidence.  

                                   Every interaction is designed to feel intuitive, helpful, and refined
                                   while reflecting the future of personalized online shopping.




Brand guidelines   Tone of voice                                                                       06
Mission                               Our mission is to expand Curate into a complete shopping
                                      ecosystem. With our extension and Shopify plugin already live and
                                      backed by $100K in funding, we are focused on building iOS and
                                      Android apps, enabling users to create avatars with body
                                      measurements, manage digital closets, and receive personalized
                                      styling. At the same time, we aim to integrate Curate across e-
                                      commerce platforms, helping businesses enhance customer
                                      experience and drive better outcomes.




Vision                                Our vision is to transform online retail into a truly personalized
                                      experience. We aim to build a dynamic ecosystem where users’
                                      body measurements, wardrobe, and style preferences power
                                      smarter recommendations. By helping customers find what fits
                                      and brands reduce returns while increasing revenue, Curate
                                      aspires to redefine how people discover and shop for clothing
                                      online.
