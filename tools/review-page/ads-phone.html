Same message, same track (Cat Walk), same 15 s, so the only difference is the motion. v3 is the first ad made with the new studio process: you said G3 was not quite up to the mark but you wanted to see the result, so here it is in motion, with its own sound.

What v3 does differently

  • One thing to look at at a time. Her jacket, then the line over her camera roll, then her market photo, then the real app, then the count, then the end card. v2 had several ideas moving at once.
  • The real app, big and long. 6.4 s of real Curate footage at about 70% of the frame: the app lifts her Ballet Flats out of the photo, then the review screen counts up to 41 pieces. In v2 the app was smaller and on screen for less time.
  • Moves with weight. Each kind of thing moves its own way: the camera glides and settles, panels slide with no bounce, words land firmly, and only Zee bounces. Played at 60 frames a second, so fast moves stay smooth.
  • A readable ending. "Curate" and "On the App Store" stay still for 2.3 s (v2: about 1 s).
  • It loops. The last frame is exactly the first, so on a replay it runs straight back into her ferry photo.
  • Its own sound, made in code on the music's beat: a soft kick on every beat, a pop when something lifts, a riser into "41" while the music dips, and a hit on the number.

Fixed since the stills (G3)

  • The review screen's quarter-second smear (8 s). It was carrying its own clock and notch, so two of each slid up the phone. It now rises under the phone's own top bar, a little slower.
  • The faint double "Curate" (14.3 s). The end card and her photo used to scroll the whole screen in 0.4 s. Now the end card lifts away and fades, then her photo rises back.
  • The phone's exit (11 s) smeared into stripes for a moment. It now drops a short way and fades as the logo arrives.
  • Kept as you left them: the flat-lay jacket cut-out (no new paid image) and the phone's position.
  • Still to judge: the hook is calmer than v1's burst, and as the jacket goes back into her photo it is half see-through for about a third of a second.
