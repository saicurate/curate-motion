Curate ads, at phone size

Each video plays in a phone-sized frame, so it looks the way people will see it on TikTok, Reels and the Instagram feed. Tap a video to play it with sound. Only one plays at a time.

Pop out: v1 vs v2

Same message, same track (Cat Walk), same 15 s. v2 follows the one-thread motion brief, so you can decide whether those become the rules for every ad.

v1Pop outCuts, whip pans, a flash and springy pops.
v2 · loopsPop out v2One continuous shot: every scene is made out of the one before.

What v2 does differently

  • No cuts, no fades. Each scene grows out of the last: her next photo floods out of a piece's own shape, the outline of her leggings becomes the scan's grid, the words collapse into a line that draws the phone, and her photo's tiles flip over into the real scan.
  • The app's own tiles do the moving. The scan's tiles fold into the Wardrobe's tiles, and her pieces lift out of the Wardrobe as the camera swings round the phone.
  • The denim jacket becomes the logo. It collapses into Curate's star, the mark draws around it, and the end title builds from there.
  • It loops. The last frame is the first frame: the camera goes through the star and lands back on her first photo.
  • Stricter look. One typeface (Geist). Black, white and three colours from her own clothes: denim, camel, sage. No flash, no bounce, no glow.
  • Something moves on every beat, and nothing holds still longer than half a second. In v1 the end card holds still for about 3½ seconds.
  • Its own sound. A soft kick on every beat, a riser into the big moment, and a small hit or whoosh on every change, all made in code, on top of the music.
  • Real motion blur on the fast moves, the way a camera would record it.
  • Trade-off: the end title is on screen for about 1 second (v1: about 3½ s), and the real app footage is shown smaller and for less time.
